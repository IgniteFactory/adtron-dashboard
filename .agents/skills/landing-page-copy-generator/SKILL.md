# Landing Page Copy Generator
## The Apple Blueprint for High-Conversion Web Copy

Generate complete, high-converting landing page copy using Apple's copywriting architecture: clarity, emotional resonance, rhythmic language, and the FAB persuasion model.

---

## Step 1: Run the Intake Interview

Before writing a single word, ask the user these questions in one message. Wait for answers before proceeding.

1. **Product/Service**: What exactly are you selling? One paragraph.
2. **Target Audience**: Who is your ideal customer? (demographics, mindset, situation)
3. **Primary Pain Point**: What is the #1 struggle before finding you?
4. **The Transformation**: What does their life look like AFTER using your product?
5. **Top 3 Features**: What are the three most compelling things about your offer?
6. **Unique Selling Point**: What makes you different from competitors?
7. **Offer Details**: Price, guarantee, bonuses, deadline — anything that reduces risk or adds urgency.
8. **Tone**: Professional / Conversational / Bold & Direct / Warm & Empathetic

If the user has already provided most of this, extract what you can and only ask for what's missing.

---

## Step 2: Apply the 15-Second Neuroscience Test (Before Writing)

Every piece of copy must pass through three cognitive gates. Use this as a mental filter when drafting each section:

| Stage | Timing | What the visitor judges | What must be right |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gut Reaction | 0–50ms | Credibility at a glance | Visual hierarchy, headline size, layout signal |
| Pattern Recognition | 0.05–3s | "Does this look legit?" | Clear headline, logical flow, trust signals |
| Relevance Assessment | 3–15s | "Is this for me?" | Headline matches intent, value prop is immediate |

**Rule:** If the page fails at any gate, the visitor leaves. Write the hero section last — after you know the full story — so you can compress the entire pitch into those first 15 seconds.

---

## Step 3: Build the Copy Architecture (The Inverted Pyramid)

Structure every page in three layers so both scanners (79% of visitors) and readers get what they need:

- **Layer 1 — The Big Message**: Huge, bold headlines with 4–5 core value propositions. Scanners leave with the gist.
- **Layer 2 — Strategic Subheads**: Tell the full story even if body copy is skipped.
- **Layer 3 — Technical Deep-Dives**: Hard facts and specs for buyers who need logic to justify the purchase.

Write all six sections below in order.

---

### SECTION 1: HERO (Above the Fold)

**Job:** Pass the 15-second test. Visitor must instantly know: what it is, who it's for, why it matters.

**Pre-headline (1 line)** — Qualify the audience immediately.
> Format: "For [audience] who [situation]..." or a bold provocative statement.

**Primary Headline (under 10 words)** — The single most important line. Communicate the "Better Life" outcome, not a feature. Apply one of these Apple techniques:
- **Contradiction**: "Small is the new powerful." / "Less to carry, more to love."
- **Single Power Words**: "Tap. Type. Swipe." — speed through brevity.
- **Sensory language**: Words that engage sight, sound, or touch.
- **The Rule of Three**: "Thinnest. Lightest. Fastest."

**Subheadline (1–2 sentences)** — Expand the headline promise. Introduce the mechanism. Use "you/your" language.

**Primary CTA Button** — Action verb + outcome. Never "Submit" or "Buy Now."
> Examples: "Start Getting Results Today" / "Claim My Free Strategy Call"

**Trust Micro-copy** (1 line below CTA) — Remove the #1 objection to clicking.
> Examples: "No credit card required." / "Join 4,200+ brands already growing."

---

### SECTION 2: PAIN AGITATION

**Job:** Make the visitor feel deeply understood. Agitate before you cure.

Use the **Emotional Trigger Matrix** — choose the right trigger for your audience:

| Category | Trigger | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Gain** | Status & Transformation | "Imagine being the person who..." / "Your new superpower." |
| **Logic** | Relief & Convenience | Specific stats, time saved, effort removed. |
| **Fear** | Loss Aversion & FOMO | What they lose by waiting. What competitors already have. |
| **Social** | Tribal Identity | Position the user as part of an elite group. Subtle sass against the old way. |

Write 3–4 short paragraphs or punchy bullets:
1. Name the pain clearly and specifically
2. Describe the consequence of NOT solving it (loss aversion)
3. Dismiss common "band-aid" solutions they've tried
4. Position your product as the real solution

> Use: "Are you tired of..." / "You've probably tried..." / "The problem isn't you — it's..."

---

### SECTION 3: THE SOLUTION REVEAL

**Job:** Introduce your product as the hero. Pivot from pain to possibility.

Write a short product introduction (2–3 paragraphs):
- Name the product and give it a clear identity
- Explain the core mechanism: HOW it solves the problem differently
- Paint the "After" picture using sensory and transformation language

Apply the **FAB Formula** for each key feature — write at least 3 FAB blocks. Always lead with the Benefit:

```
Feature:   [The technical component]
Advantage: [What it does / how it works]
Benefit:   [The emotional outcome — the "So what?" asked twice]
```

**The "So What?" Test:** Ask it twice.
- Feature: "14,000mAh battery"
- So what? → "Lasts all day without charging"
- So what? → "You stay focused and in control — not hunting for an outlet"

Present as benefit-led bullets. The benefit is the headline; the feature is the proof.

---

### SECTION 4: SOCIAL PROOF

**Job:** Remove doubt with evidence from real people.

Write 3 testimonial frameworks structured for maximum impact:
- **Opening**: A specific result or transformation (never "great product!")
- **Middle**: What life was like BEFORE
- **End**: What changed AFTER, with one specific detail

Also write:
- A **stats bar** (3 numbers that build credibility — user fills in real data)
  > Example: "4,200+ customers | 94% satisfaction | ₹2.3Cr revenue generated"
- A **logos strip label** if applicable: "Trusted by teams at..."

---

### SECTION 5: OBJECTION HANDLING (FAQ)

**Job:** Answer the 4–5 questions a skeptical buyer is silently asking.

Write 4–5 FAQ items that pre-empt the most common objections. Frame questions the way a real skeptic would ask them. Address:
- "Is this right for me?" (specificity)
- "Will this actually work?" (proof / mechanism)
- "What if it doesn't work?" (guarantee / risk reversal)
- "Is now the right time?" (urgency / scarcity)
- "How is this different?" (USP reinforcement)

---

### SECTION 6: CLOSING CTA

**Job:** Convert the high-intent visitor who has read to the bottom.

Write:

**Closing Headline** — Restate the transformation with urgency. Use contradiction or repetition for rhythm.

**Offer Stack Summary** — Bulleted recap of everything included (product + bonuses + guarantee). Make the value undeniable.

**Risk Reversal Line** — One sentence that removes the final barrier.
> "If you don't see results in 30 days, we'll refund every penny. No questions asked."

**Final CTA Button** — Same as primary CTA or a slightly more urgent variant.

**Final Micro-copy** — Reinforce safety or urgency one last time.

## Step 4: Apply Apple Linguistic Polish

After drafting all sections, read every headline and subhead aloud. Then apply:

### Rhythm & Sound Devices
| Device | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule of Three** | Always group benefits in triplets | "Easy. Intuitive. Delightful." |
| **Alliteration** | Repeat initial sounds in bullets | "Fast, flexible, foolproof." |
| **Rhyme** | Use in short punchy lines | "Chill if you spill." |
| **Purposeful Repetition** | Repeat power words with a beat | "Your photo. Your font. Your brand." |
| **Contradiction** | Pair opposites in one line | "Less effort. More revenue." |
| **Single Verbs** | Replace phrases with one strong verb | "Tap. Build. Launch." |

### Voice & Focus Rules
- **You/Your dominance**: Count them. "You/Your" should appear at least 2x more than the brand name.
- **Outcome over features**: Always lead with what the customer gains.
- **Specificity over vagueness**: "14 days" beats "fast". "$47,000 saved" beats "save money".
- **Brevity**: Cut any sentence that doesn't earn its place. The shorter, the stronger.

### Sensory Language Checklist
Before finalising, ensure the copy engages at least two senses:
- **Sight**: "Crystal-clear", "vivid", "invisible"
- **Touch**: "Smooth", "seamless", "frictionless"
- **Sound**: "Silent", "crisp", "clicks into place"
- **Motion**: "Flies", "glides", "snaps"

## Core Principles — Never Violate

- **Pain before cure**: Never reveal the solution before the reader feels understood
- **Outcome over features**: Lead with what the customer gains, not what the product does
- **One CTA per section**: Never give the reader two things to click at once
- **You/Your ratio**: If the brand name appears more than "you", rewrite
- **The Inverted Pyramid**: Big message first, details last — always
- **Read aloud test**: If it doesn't flow when spoken, it won't convert when read
